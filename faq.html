Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about EverBloom Studio preserved boxwood wreaths and botanicals: real plants, wholesale, minimum order quantities, samples, sizes, handmade pieces, and how to contact us.

Are preserved boxwood wreaths real plants?

Yes. EverBloom's preserved botanicals are real boxwood, hydrangea and seasonal botanicals, preserved to keep their color and shape without water or sunlight. They are never faux or plastic.

How long do preserved boxwood wreaths last?

Preserved boxwood keeps its color and shape without water or sunlight. How long a piece lasts depends on how it is handled and displayed; EverBloom gives care guidance with a sample or wholesale order.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes. Wholesale inquiries are open for the preserved botanical line. Email info@everbloomstudio.us for the wholesale price list, minimum order quantities and sample terms.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the item and is quoted per order. Ask for it by email at info@everbloomstudio.us — it is not published on the gallery page.

How do I order samples?

Select pieces on the Preserved Botanicals gallery page (https://everbloomstudio.us/preserved-botanicals) with “Add to inquiry” and send the list in one email, or email info@everbloomstudio.us directly. Sample price and shipping are confirmed before payment.

What sizes are available?

Sizes are printed on each catalog page. Small pieces run 6–16 inches (SSC series). The 2026 collection is 99 pages and the 2025 collection is 35 pages, two pieces per page.

Are the pieces handmade?

Yes. The pieces are handmade at the partner workshop in Lingyuan, Liaoning, China, which has made preserved boxwood and hydrangea pieces for three years and has supplied TJ Maxx in the United States since 2025.

Where is EverBloom Studio based?

EverBloom Studio LLC is based in Brooklyn, New York. Website: https://everbloomstudio.us/ — Email: info@everbloomstudio.us.
