GolfSwing AI 隐私政策

运营者：EverBloom Studio LLC

简而言之：你的视频留在手机里；为了 AI 诊断，只上传 4 张关键帧和动作数据；我们不会出售你的数据。

1. 视频

你选择的视频只在手机本地处理，使用设备端姿态识别（Google MediaPipe）。视频不会被上传。

2. 我们会发送什么

当你请求 AI 诊断时，我们会发送：由 4 张关键帧拼成、带骨骼标注的一张图片；手机计算出的动作数据；你选择的球杆和机位；你填写的资料（惯用手、水平、差点、常见失误、练习目标）；App 语言；一个随机生成的设备编号。

3. 我们的服务器与 AI 服务商

请求经由我们的服务器（部署在 Cloudflare）转发给 AI 服务商（Google Gemini 或 DeepSeek）生成诊断。我们的服务器不保存你的图片；AI 服务商按其自身条款处理这些数据。

4. 设备编号与使用次数

设备编号是 App 随机生成的数字，不是手机的硬件标识。我们用它统计每天的诊断次数（保留 2 天）和记录你的会员状态。

5. 支付

支付由 Google Play、微信支付或支付宝处理。我们只会收到订单或订阅状态，不会获得你的银行卡或账户信息。国内版集成了支付宝 SDK 和微信 OpenSDK，仅在你选择付款时使用，并按其各自的隐私政策处理数据。

6. 历史记录与删除

历史记录（关键帧图片和诊断结果）只保存在你的手机上。你可以在 App 内删除任意记录，卸载 App 会清除全部数据。如需删除我们服务器上的会员或订单记录，请联系我们。

7. 联系我们

邮箱：info@everbloomstudio.us

GolfSwing AI Privacy Policy

Operator: EverBloom Studio LLC

In short: your videos stay on your phone. Only 4 key frames and motion data are sent for AI analysis, and we don't sell your data.

1. Videos

Videos you choose are processed only on your phone, using on-device pose detection (Google MediaPipe). Videos are never uploaded.

2. What we send

When you request an AI analysis, we send: one image made of 4 key frames with a skeleton overlay; the motion numbers computed on your phone; the club and camera angle you chose; the profile you filled in (handedness, level, handicap, common miss, goal); your app language; and a random device ID.

3. Our server and AI providers

Requests go through our server (hosted on Cloudflare) to an AI provider (Google Gemini or DeepSeek), which returns the analysis. Our server does not store your images. The AI provider processes them under its own terms.

4. Device ID and usage counts

The device ID is a random number created by the app. It is not your phone's hardware ID. We use it to count daily analyses (kept for 2 days) and to remember your membership.

5. Payments

Payments are handled by Google Play, WeChat Pay or Alipay. We receive only the order or subscription status, never your card or account details. The Chinese version includes the Alipay SDK and WeChat OpenSDK, used only when you choose to pay; they process data under their own privacy policies.

6. Your history and deletion

Your history (key-frame images and results) is stored only on your phone. You can delete any record in the app, and uninstalling removes everything. To delete membership or order records on our server, contact us.
