A printable PDF, with the scale on the page

These are paper patterns. Each file is an instant-download PDF. Print it yourself. The pictures lower on this page are watermarked samples. The full PDF, without the SAMPLE mark, is delivered after purchase.

  • Print at 100%

    Choose actual size. Do not use “fit to page,” “shrink to fit,” or a custom scale. The pieces are drawn to size.

  • Check the test square

    Every pattern sheet prints a 1-inch square and a 3 cm square. Measure them. If they are not those sizes, the clothes will not fit.

  • Seam allowances included

    The allowance is on the pieces, not something you add. The sheets mark a 1/4-inch seam allowance.

  • Pieces, layout, and steps

    Full-size pattern pieces, a cutting layout, and illustrated sewing steps. The skirt in the sundress is a measured rectangle, not a shaped piece.

No doll brand is named here, on purpose. EverBloom Studio is independent. These patterns are not licensed by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any doll manufacturer. The fit is described only as most 18-inch dolls. Bodies differ. Measure your doll, and check the test square, before you cut.

Fabric, and how you use the PDF

Light cotton works for all three. Small-scale florals and solids in cream, leaf green, and clay sit with the rest of the studio. Quilting cotton is a sound everyday choice. This page does not link out to fabric shops. Yardage is printed in the PDF, not guessed here.

  1. 1

    Print at 100% (actual size). Do not scale the page to fit the paper.

  2. 2

    Measure the test square. It must be 1 inch, and the metric square must be 3 cm.

  3. 3

    Cut the paper pieces. Seam allowances are already included.

  4. 4

    Cut the fabric from the layout in the PDF, then sew in the order the steps give.

  5. 5

    Fit the garment on an 18-inch doll. If your doll is outside a typical 18-inch body, the pattern may not fit.

Small snaps and buttons are easy to lose. If a young child plays with the dressed doll, keep extra notions out of reach. The license for personal sewing, and whether finished clothes may be sold, is printed in the PDF.

Questions

What does “fits most 18-inch dolls” mean?

The pieces are drafted for dolls about 18 inches tall. Bodies are not all the same. The patterns do not name a doll brand, and they are not sized to one manufacturer’s body. Measure your doll, print at 100%, and check the test square before you cut.

Are the patterns tied to a doll brand?

No. EverBloom Studio is independent. These patterns are not licensed by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any doll manufacturer. A Peter Pan collar is a rounded collar style. It is not a character name and not a brand.

How do I print the PDF?

It is a printable PDF. Print at 100% actual size. Measure the 1-inch test square and the 3 cm square on the sheet. Seam allowances are included on the pieces and marked as 1/4 inch. If the test square is the wrong size, do not cut fabric.
