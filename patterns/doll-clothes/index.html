What “fits 18-inch dolls” means

These patterns are being drafted for dolls about 18 inches tall, with about these measurements. Doll bodies are not all the same. When a PDF launches, this chart will be printed in it so you can measure before you cut.

Draft measurements for the 18-inch doll clothes
Measurement Draft target
Height 18 in
Chest 11 1/4 in
Waist 10 1/2 in
Hips 12 in

No doll brand is named here, on purpose. EverBloom Studio is independent. These patterns are not licensed by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any doll manufacturer. If a doll is outside this chart, the pattern may not fit. Measure, and wait for the PDF before you cut.

Fabric, notions, and a small-parts note

Choose light cotton or linen. Small-scale florals and solids in cream, leaf green, and clay will sit with the rest of the studio. Quilting cotton is a sound everyday choice. This page does not link out to fabric shops.

  • Printer, US Letter paper, tape or a glue stick for tiled pages, and paper scissors.
  • Fabric shears, pins or clips, and thread to match.
  • Snaps, or small buttons if you prefer them. Sew closures down firmly.
  • Narrow elastic for the bloomers.
  • A sewing machine, or a hand needle if you would rather sew it that way.

Small snaps and buttons are easy to lose. If a young child plays with the dressed doll, keep extra notions out of reach.

How you will use the PDF

  1. 1

    Print the pages at 100 percent scale on US Letter. Do not use “fit to page.”

  2. 2

    Tape tiled sheets on the marks, then cut the paper pieces.

  3. 3

    Cut the fabric from the layout, and transfer any marks the sheet names.

  4. 4

    Sew in the order the guide gives, with the allowance printed on the pieces.

  5. 5

    Fit the garment on an 18-inch doll and hem to the length you want.
