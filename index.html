From the workshop bench to Brooklyn

EverBloom Studio LLC was founded in New York in 2026 on a simple frustration: most “everlasting” home décor is plastic pretending to be a plant. We only work with the real thing — boxwood and flowers that grew in soil, leather that came from a hide, clay that was shaped and painted by hand.

Our preserved botanicals come from our partner workshop in Lingyuan, Liaoning, which has made real preserved wreaths for three years and now supplies major U.S. retailers. Our leather, ceramic and cross-stitch lines are sourced the same way: finished goods from small workshops, tested piece by piece in Brooklyn before anything is listed for sale.

Beside those finished goods, two pattern lines are on the drawing table: floral hand-embroidery PDFs, and sewing patterns for doll clothes that fit 18-inch dolls. They are not for sale yet. When the files are ready, you will print them at home.
